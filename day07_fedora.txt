from user then calculate life path number.(use switch cate)

	date: 23
	
	calculation for life path number:
						a=date%10

						c=a+b
	
	if life path number:
					1:Lucky
					2:carefuly do your work
					3.storger
					4.Happy
					5.can get help
					6.Doubt

					9.Courage

echo "Enter the date of birth: "
read date
a=$(($date %10))
b=$((date /10))
c=$(($a+$b))
case $c in
1) echo "Lucky" ;;
2) echo "Carefuly do your work" ;;
3) echo "Storger" ;;
4) echo "Happy" ;;
5) echo "can get help" ;;
6) echo "Doubt" ;;
7) echo "Sad" ;;
8) echo "Like" ;;
9) echo "Courage" ;;
*) echo "Invalid" ;;
esac

Q2).Give list of numbers then calculate the summation and multiplication
using for loop.
Example:-
	1 2 3 4 5
		summation = 15
		multiplication = 120
		
[nuwani@LAPTOP-2OMR359V ~]$ vi Qu2.sh
[nuwani@LAPTOP-2OMR359V ~]$ chmod 777 Qu2.sh
[nuwani@LAPTOP-2OMR359V ~]$ ./Qu2.sh
Enter numbers separated by space:
1 2 3 4 5
Summation: 15
Multiplication: 120
[nuwani@LAPTOP-2OMR359V ~]$ more Qu2.sh
sum=0
mul=1
echo "Enter numbers separated by space: "
read -a numbers
for num in "${numbers[@]}"
do
sum=$(($sum+num))
mul=$(($mul*num))
done
echo "Summation: $sum"
echo "Multiplication: $mul"
